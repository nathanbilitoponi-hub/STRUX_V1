\# STRUX Public Claims



\## Status



This document defines what STRUX can and cannot claim publicly at the current stage.



STRUX is an experimental structural diagnostics framework.



It should be presented conservatively.



\---



\## Public-safe positioning



STRUX can currently be described as:



```text

An experimental geometric corridor diagnostics framework for spatial networks.



or:



A structural analysis framework for detecting supported corridors, fragile passages, choke risks, and continuity patterns in spatial graphs.

Public-safe claims



The following claims are acceptable:



STRUX analyzes structural continuity in spatial networks.

STRUX can score whether an apparent connection has geometric support.

STRUX can help identify corridor-like structures.

STRUX can compare geometric support against standard graph baselines.

STRUX can detect choke-prone or fragile passages in experimental settings.

STRUX is being tested on synthetic benchmarks and real spatial networks.

STRUX is currently strongest on corridor diagnostics, transport geometry, clearance constraints, and structural fragility experiments.

Claims to avoid publicly



Do not publicly claim that STRUX:



proves new physics

discovers a fourth physical dimension

explains cosmology

replaces topological data analysis

replaces standard graph theory

proves universal structural laws

proves observer compression as a universal law

proves SDSS/cosmic structure claims

proves that 0.5 / 1 / 1.5 / 3 are physical constants



These ideas may remain internal research hypotheses, but they are not public product claims.



Public/product track



Public-facing STRUX work should focus on:



public\_applications/

validated benchmarks

corridor diagnostics

choke risk

clearance

OSM / floorplans / spatial networks



Current public-facing example:



public\_applications/corridor\_diagnostics/osm/florence\_osm/

Preferred short description

product claim = corridor diagnostics
